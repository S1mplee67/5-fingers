## 🌟 Overview

**5 F!NGERS** is a fast, 100% offline computer touch-typing tutor and developer syntax training app. Created by **S1mplee67 (W3NCY?)**, it combines the disciplined pedagogical multi-stage progression of **[TypingClub](https://www.typingclub.com/)** with modern telemetry, microsecond caret physics, and dark aesthetic themes.

Unlike standard typing websites that require constant internet connections, logins, or paid subscription tiers, **5 F!NGERS** runs directly on your Windows PC with **zero external dependencies, zero sign-in, and zero tracking**, while persistently saving all your progress, stars, WPM improvement charts, and finger mistake heatmaps locally.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🖐 Multi-Stage Finger School (10 Comprehensive Stages)
- **Pedagogical Progression**: Starts slowly with single fingers and anchors (`F` and `J` physical bumps) before expanding across 10 progressive stages:
  - **Stage 1**: Home Row Foundation (Index Anchors F & J, D & K, S & L, A & ;, Space Discipline, Mastery Words)
  - **Stage 2**: Top Row & Upward Reaches (E & I, R & U, T & Y, W & O, Q & P)
  - **Stage 3**: Bottom Row & Downward Reaches (C & ,, V & M, B & N, X & ., Z & /)
  - **Stage 4**: Capitalization & Dual Shifts (Opposite Pinky Discipline)
  - **Stage 5**: Number Row Mastery (Home-to-Number Reaches)
  - **Stage 6**: Punctuation & Developer Symbols (Brackets `[]`, Braces `{}`, Semicolons `;`, Quotes, Operators)
  - **Stage 7**: Core Bigrams & Rhythm Flow (`th`, `he`, `in`, `er`, `an`, `on`, double-letters)
  - **Stage 8**: Developer Operators & Syntax Glyphs (`=>`, `===`, `!==`, `&&`, `||`, ternaries, chaining)
  - **Stage 9**: Real-World Architecture & Flow (Async/await, OOP classes, TypeScript interfaces)
  - **Stage 10**: Grandmaster Endurance Marathon (Multi-sentence stamina and rhythm crucible)
- **Strict Accuracy Gate**: Lessons require **≥90% accuracy** to unlock the next level, cementing muscle memory before speed.
- **Star Rating System**: Earn 1 to 5 stars on every lesson based on your combined speed and accuracy.

### 2. 👐 Redesigned Anatomical Vector Hands & 75% Mechanical Keyboard
- **Sleek Anatomical Hands**: Ergonomic hand silhouettes in top-down resting position over the anchor keys with natural wrist curves, knuckle joint accents, and glowing fingertip neon sensor pads.
- **Authentic 75% Mechanical Keyboard Layout**: 84-key ANSI layout with standard Function Row (`Esc`, `F1-F12`), compact navigation column (`Del`, `Home`, `PgUp`, `PgDn`, `End`), dedicated arrow cluster (`▲`, `▼`, `◄`, `►`), tactile bumps on `F` & `J`, and balanced 16u row grid.
- **Dual-Shift Rules**: Enforces proper touch-typing shift discipline (Left pinky holds Shift for right-hand keys; Right pinky holds Shift for left-hand keys).

### 3. 💻 Developer Code Arena (Gated by Stage 1 Mastery)
- **Stage 1 Unlock Requirement**: To prevent bad habits, the Code Arena is locked until you complete all 6 lessons of Stage 1 (Home Row Foundation) with at least 90% accuracy.
- Train real-world coding muscle memory across 10 programming languages (brackets `{}`, parentheses `()`, indents, semicolons `;`, arrow functions `=>`, operators `===`):
- **JavaScript / TypeScript** (ES6 async/await, functional pipelines, classes)
- **Python 3** (Comprehensions, context managers, algorithms)
- **C# / .NET** (LINQ queries, generics, pattern matching)
- **C & C++** (STL algorithms, lambdas, pointers)
- **Java** (Streams API, try-with-resources, multithreading)
- **Rust** (Result matching, traits, ownership syntax)
- **Go / Golang** (Goroutines, channels, HTTP handlers, struct tags)
- **SQL** (GROUP BY, HAVING, multi-table INNER JOINs, window functions)
- **HTML5 & CSS3** (Flexbox components, custom properties, responsive grids)
- **Bash / Linux Shell** (Pipes `grep | awk | sort`, flags, scripts)

### 4. ⚡ High-Precision Telemetry & 3-Tier Speed Sprints
- Butter-smooth animated caret with sub-millisecond input responsiveness.
- Live real-time meters for **WPM, Raw WPM, Accuracy %, and Consistency %**.
- **3 Sprint Options**: Choose between **Short (15 words)** rapid bursts, **Medium (30 words)** standard tests, or **Long (60 words)** endurance sprints.

### 5. 💡 Glowing Wisdom Lightbulb & 36 RNG Tips Engine
- Animated golden/amber neon pulsing lightbulb with interactive scale-glow on hover.
- Interactive **🎲 Roll Tip** system with 36 curated touch-typing, ergonomics, switch mechanics, and programming syntax tips.
- Instant, non-intrusive re-roll on click with smooth text fade transitions.

### 6. 🔊 Synthetic Mechanical Keyboard Audio (Zero External Files)
Synthesized live via the Web Audio API without needing external sound samples:
- **Cherry MX Blue**: Crisp tactile click.
- **Cherry MX Red**: Smooth linear clack.
- **Cherry MX Brown**: Subdued tactile bump.
- **Vintage Typewriter**: Heavy mechanical thud with completion carriage bell chime.
- **Mute**: Pure silent mode for quiet environments.

### 7. 🎨 Hand-Crafted Visual Themes
- **Cyber Slate (Default)**: Deep carbon slate with tactical cyan accents.
- **Midnight Obsidian (Eye Relief)**: Pure OLED dark with warm sepia parchment text for late-night typing sessions.
- **Nordic Frost**: Cold graphite with arctic blue highlights.
- **Retro Phosphor**: Classic terminal phosphor green.

### 8. 📊 Offline Analytics & 10-Finger Error Heatmaps
- Interactive 2D canvas curve tracking your WPM speed over time.
- 10-finger error heatmap visually pinpointing your weakest fingers.
- Top mistyped key badges (e.g. `p`, `b`, `;`).
- 1-Click **JSON Export & Import** backup to save or transfer your progress anytime.

## 🛠️ Quick Start (Windows)

No installation or node modules required!

### Option 1: Instant Launcher
Double-click:
```bat
Run-5FINGERS.bat
```

### Option 2: Standalone Windows Executable
If you want to compile the native C# desktop launcher executable (`5-FINGERS.exe`), run:
```bat
build.bat
```
*(Uses the Windows built-in .NET Framework compiler `csc.exe` — zero downloads required!)*
